# Course recommendation: source credibility vs user suitability

## Principle
Two separate dimensions must not be conflated:

1. **Source-grounded identity (eligibility):** A specific course name and provider must have matching literal evidence from the cited course URL, with at least one verified educational detail (topic, level, or duration). A fabricated or mismatched course is never allowed into the final ranking. Unsupported optional claims are removed.
2. **Learning fit (graded):** A source-backed course may fit a user at 80%, 60%, etc. based on topic (35%), learning level (25%), goal (25%), and preferred pedagogy (15%). Scores are heuristic estimates, not empirical probabilities or provider-quality ratings.

Unknown audience, duration or price never automatically disqualifies a course unless a matching **hard** constraint requires that fact (e.g. free-only budget and unknown price). Show missing fields in the report. Unknown dimensions get neutral 3/5 in the Agent rubric unless there is clearer evidence; do not treat missing facts as affirmative matches.

The report now displays the match percentage, reason for suitability, known limitations, and unknown fields. An approximate 80% match is legitimate, and no course must be a 100% match to be recommended.

## MVP limitation
A completed report still requires six distinct named providers. Missing sources are not fabricated to fill the list. Current course discovery and evidence matching still require live quality evaluation.

**Identity integrity:** a fabricated or mismatched course-name/provider quote is explicitly rejected, even while optional fields may remain unknown.
